Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Overview

Steam Stats (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is an open-source Steam companion dashboard for exploring public Steam profile and library data. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use Steam Stats.

Information we handle

  • Steam identifiers you provide. When you search, you may enter a SteamID64, profile URL, or vanity name. That input is sent to our backend so we can resolve and load Steam profile and library data.
  • Steam profile and library data. Public Steam information returned by the Steam Web API / Store APIs (such as persona name, avatar, owned games, and playtime) is fetched by our backend and shown in the app.
  • Local session preference. The browser may store the last resolved Steam ID in localStorage so your session can be restored after a refresh. This value stays on your device unless you clear it in Settings or clear site data.
  • Optional local caches. The app may cache some game store details in browser storage for a short period to improve performance.
  • Technical logs. The host running a Steam Stats instance may keep standard server logs (for example timestamps and request metadata) for security and reliability.

Accounts

Steam Stats does not currently offer user accounts or Steam OpenID sign-in. Looking up a profile does not create an account with us.

How information is used

  • To resolve Steam identifiers and display profile, library, statistics, and related dashboard features.
  • To keep a local session so you do not have to re-enter a Steam ID after refreshing.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the service (including rate limiting and diagnosing errors).

Steam API and third parties

Steam data is retrieved through our backend using Steam’s APIs. Steam’s handling of data is governed by Valve / Steam policies. Steam Stats is not affiliated with Valve Corporation. We do not sell personal data.

Data retention and deletion

Server-side responses may be cached briefly for performance. You can clear the active Steam profile from Settings, which removes the stored Steam ID from your browser. Clearing browser site data removes local caches as well.

Self-hosted deployments

Steam Stats is open source and may be hosted by third parties. If you use a self-hosted or third-party instance, that operator is responsible for how data is stored, secured, and processed on their infrastructure.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.
